## Identity rings (holdout)

Generated by [`server/scripts/ringEval.ts`](../server/scripts/ringEval.ts) from `data/raw-sample` (synthetic development sample, not results).
Rings link card entities and device entities seen in the same transaction. Prior fraud comes from training-period labels only.
Holdout: 13,264 transactions, fraud rate 2.87%.

| Supernode cap | Supernodes cut | Largest ring | Flagged tx (size ≥3 + prior fraud) | Fraud rate when flagged | Lift | Fraud caught | Lift, prior fraud on another entity only |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 0 | 589 | 2,120 (15.98%) | 10.90% | 3.8x | 60.63% | 3.8x (2,120 tx) |
| 50 | 6 | 29 | 308 (2.32%) | 72.40% | 25.2x | 58.53% | 25.2x (308 tx) |

Without the cap, generic device strings merge most device-bearing customers into one ring, so "in a risky ring" stops meaning anything. The last column excludes the transaction's own card entity, separating the ring signal from a plain blocklist hit.
