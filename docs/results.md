## Identity rings (holdout)

Generated by [`server/scripts/ringEval.ts`](../server/scripts/ringEval.ts) from `data/raw`.
Rings link card entities and device entities seen in the same transaction. Prior fraud comes from training-period labels only.
Holdout: 118,534 transactions, fraud rate 3.44%.

| Supernode cap | Supernodes cut | Largest ring | Flagged tx (size ≥3 + prior fraud) | Fraud rate when flagged | Lift | Fraud caught | Lift, prior fraud on another entity only |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 0 | 63,586 | 22,742 (19.19%) | 8.26% | 2.4x | 46.11% | 2.4x (22,733 tx) |
| 50 | 245 | 22,598 | 12,649 (10.67%) | 10.01% | 2.9x | 31.08% | 2.9x (12,633 tx) |
| 20 | 589 | 10,042 | 7,573 (6.39%) | 14.04% | 4.1x | 26.10% | 4.1x (7,543 tx) |
| 10 | 1099 | 5,003 | 5,438 (4.59%) | 15.08% | 4.4x | 20.13% | 4.3x (5,383 tx) |
| 5 | 1919 | 918 | 2,721 (2.30%) | 16.13% | 4.7x | 10.78% | 4.6x (2,638 tx) |

Without the cap, generic device strings merge most device-bearing customers into one ring, so "in a risky ring" stops meaning anything. The API uses a cap of 50 (DEGREE_CAP); smaller caps are shown to describe the trade-off and were not chosen on this holdout. The last column excludes the transaction's own card entity, separating the ring signal from a plain blocklist hit.

## Operating point chosen by cost

Generated by [`ml/costs.py`](../ml/costs.py) on `data/raw`.
